Keeper

Sift a whole card before your coffee cools.

Keeper is a Mac app for culling camera photos with the keyboard. Arrow through, delete the misses, send the keepers to Photos.

Load once

A short wait, then no waiting.

Plug in the card. Keeper reads every photo once and keeps a sharp preview on your Mac, so each arrow press is instant. Open the card again and it's ready at once.

←→⇧→R⌫

Keyboard first

Every key does one thing.

Arrows to move. Shift to select a run. R to rotate, losslessly. Delete sends the shot and its RAW to the Trash, and ⌘Z brings it back.

On-device smarts

It spots the misses for you.

Keeper checks every shot for blur and bad exposure, on your Mac. Press F to see only the flagged ones, select them all, and delete in one go.

Share the keepers

Straight into Photos.

Select the good ones and press P to add them to your library, or ⇧P for a new album ready to share as a Shared Album. Or send them with AirDrop or Messages.
